02 / SCOPE

What counts as crypto?

How findings are matched

We inspect every CSV field, including finding names, descriptions, package names and asset metadata. Strong indicators (such as “weak cipher”, “CVE-2023-…”, “xmrig” or “mining pool”) are included. General terms such as “crypto” or “OpenSSL” are included as supporting indicators. This is a keyword filter, not a severity assessment; review matches in context.
